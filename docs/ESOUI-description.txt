[B]Know what you have and where it is.[/B] Every character's bags and gear, the bank, the craft bag, house storage, the Furniture Vault, furniture placed in your houses, and your currencies. See it all on a summary page with running currency totals, copy it in game, or export it to Excel.
[QUOTE][SIZE="3"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Get your data out of the game[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
ESO gives you no list of what you own. Here, the [B]copy window[/B] puts every item on every character, in the bank, craft bag, coffers, Furniture Vault and houses into text that pastes straight into Excel columns, and the [B]Excel export[/B] writes it all to one CSV with 24 columns.[/QUOTE]
[LIST]
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Nothing to switch on.[/B][/COLOR]  It reads quietly in the background and never opens a window unless you ask. The only chat line it posts by itself is a one-time welcome on a new install, pointing you to [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger[/B][/FONT].
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Read only.[/B][/COLOR]  It never moves, sells or changes an item. It only reads what the game shows you.
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Your data stays home.[/B][/COLOR]  Saved per account and megaserver on your PC. Nothing leaves it.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Getting started[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Install [B]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/B] and [B]Fury's Addons Menu[/B] (see Requires above).
[*]Log in on each character once. Open each house coffer and the Furniture Vault once, and visit the houses you own. The summary page lists what is [B]Still to read[/B], so you do not have to remember.
[*]Type [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger[/B][/FONT] for the summary, or bind a key under Controls > Addon Keybinds > Fury's Inventory Ledger.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]What it lists[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Each character:[/B] backpack, equipped gear, the summoned companion's gear, and the character's wallet (gold, Alliance Points, Tel Var, writ vouchers).
[*][B]Account-wide:[/B] bank (including the ESO Plus half), craft bag, banked currencies, and account currencies such as transmute crystals and Undaunted keys.
[*][B]House storage[/B] (each coffer by its name or nickname) and the [B]Furniture Vault[/B].
[*][B]Placed furniture[/B], counted per house, for houses you own.
[*]Identical stacks in the same place are added together. Stolen copies get their own row.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]When it reads[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]At login, and a moment after anything in your bags, bank or craft bag changes.
[*]House storage and the Furniture Vault: when you open and close them. Open each one once after installing.
[*]Placed furniture: when you enter a house you own, and after you place or remove furniture there.
[*]Other characters only update when you log in on them.
[*]The summary page has a [B]Still to read[/B] section that lists what is missing (characters, coffers, the Furniture Vault, houses) and how to add it. It disappears when nothing is left.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Summary page[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger[/B][/FONT][/I][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]Find:[/B] type part of an item or set name in the Find box to see how many you own and where, on every character and account read so far. Clear the box to go back. [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger find <words>[/B][/FONT] does the same in chat.
[*][B]Tiles:[/B] total items, different items, stacks, gold, vendor value and characters read, with the change since yesterday.
[*][B]Currencies:[/B] every currency on characters and in the bank (account-wide ones count as bank), with the total and how much it changed since yesterday and over the last 7 days. Currencies update the moment they change.
[*][B]Where it is:[/B] items, stacks, vendor value and share for each place: characters, companions, bank, craft bag, house storage, Furniture Vault and placed furniture.
[*][B]Gold over time:[/B] a bar for each saved day, up to 120.
[*][B]Space left:[/B] used and free slots in the bank, each coffer and the Furniture Vault, and each character's free bag slots. Nearly full shows in amber, full in rose.
[*][B]By item type[/B] and [B]by quality:[/B] how many of each you own.
[*][B]Split stacks:[/B] stackable items kept in more than one place, with the count in each.
[*][B]Gear by set:[/B] your sets by number of pieces, and who holds them.
[*][B]Most owned:[/B] your top 25 items by count, and where you keep them.
[*][B]Characters:[/B] items carried and equipped, gold, and when each was last read. Characters not read yet are listed too, and a deleted character is marked with the command that removes it.
[/LIST]
The change columns compare against a total saved once a day (at logout, reload, zoning and when you open the summary), so they fill in from your second day. If there is no total from exactly yesterday or 7 days ago, the heading shows the date it compares to instead. Totals are kept for 120 days. Rises show in green, drops in rose, and gold in gold with the game's coin icon.

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Copy window[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger copy[/B][/FONT], or Export / Copy on the summary[/I][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]Click [B]Select All[/B], press Ctrl+C, and paste into Excel. Tab-separated text pastes straight into columns.
[*]The [B]List[/B] picker narrows it to this account, one character or one kind of place, and [B]Type[/B] to one item type.
[*]Large lists are split into pages. Paste each page under the last.
[*]The copy window leaves out the Item Link column to keep pages small. The Excel export has it.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Export to Excel[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I]Windows, separate download. The addon works fully in game without it.[/I][/COLOR]
[LIST=1]
[*]In game, click [B]Prepare the Excel file[/B] on the summary or under [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger settings[/B][/FONT] (or type [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger export[/B][/FONT]). It builds the full list and asks before it reloads the interface, because the game only writes its saved data when it reloads or you log out.
[*]Download the [B][URL="https://github.com/AlbertHoffpauir/FurysInventoryLedger/raw/main/downloads/FurysInventoryLedger-WindowsTools-1.5.0.zip"]Windows tools zip[/URL][/B] and unzip it anywhere, for example Documents\Elder Scrolls Online. It is a separate download because ESOUI addon zips may only hold addon files. In game, the [B]Get the Windows tools[/B] button under [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger settings[/B][/FONT] (or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger tools[/B][/FONT]) opens the download page in your browser.
[*]Double-click [B]ExportToExcel.cmd[/B]. It writes [B]ESO Inventory <date time>.csv[/B] to your Desktop and opens it.
[/LIST]
[B]Keep the Excel file ready[/B] is a setting that starts turned off. When it is on, the full list is saved again when you log out, switch characters or reload and anything you own changed, so step 1 is not needed; it about doubles the size of the addon's saved file. If you used the addon before 1.5.0 it is on for you, as before.
Tip: right-click [B]ExportToExcel.cmd[/B] > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut) to run it from your desktop.
[B]What the script does[/B]
[SPOILER]
The .cmd and .ps1 files in the Windows tools zip are plain-text scripts (batch and PowerShell) you can open in Notepad and read. They only read the addon's own saved data file on your PC and write the CSV. They never connect to the internet and never touch the game's files. Nothing runs unless you start it. Source: [URL="https://github.com/AlbertHoffpauir/FurysInventoryLedger"]GitHub[/URL].
[/SPOILER]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Settings, commands and keys[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[B]Settings:[/B] the gear on the summary or the copy window, Settings > Addons > Fury's Inventory Ledger, or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger settings[/B][/FONT]. Open the summary, pick the copy window separator (tab or comma), open the copy window, rescan now, prepare the Excel file, keep the Excel file ready, or show what you own on item tooltips (one line such as "You own 412: Bank 200, Craft Bag 212"; starts turned off).
[B]Keys:[/B] Controls > Addon Keybinds > Fury's Inventory Ledger has "Open/close the summary" and "Open/close the copy window". Both start unbound.
[LIST]
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger[/B][/FONT] open the summary
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger find <words>[/B][/FONT] where an item or set is, across every character and account
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger copy[/B][/FONT] open the copy window
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger export[/B][/FONT] prepare the full Excel file for the Windows tools (asks before it reloads)
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger scan[/B][/FONT] read everything that can be read right now
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger chars[/B][/FONT] characters scanned so far, and the ones still missing
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger forget <name>[/B][/FONT] remove a deleted character
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger sep tab[/B][/FONT] | [FONT="Courier New"][B]comma[/B][/FONT] separator for the copy window
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger settings[/B][/FONT] the settings page
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/ledger tools[/B][/FONT] opens the Windows tools download page in your browser
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Questions[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[B]I play two accounts at once (two game clients). Why is one missing items?[/B]
Each client writes its own copy of the saved data at logout or /reloadui, so the client that saves last wins. Let one client log out before the other logs in.

[B]Does it see my other accounts?[/B]
Yes. Every account that plays on this PC saves into the same file and shows up in the export after it logs in once with the addon on.

[B]What language are the names in?[/B]
Item names, qualities and types come from the game in your client's language. Column headings are in English.
